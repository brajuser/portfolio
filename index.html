About

Hello, I'm Bodh Raj.

I studied BA Economics and fell for the part of marketing you can measure. I started as an SEO executive at Websoftrix Infotech and grew into Digital Marketing Manager and Team Lead, and delivered SEO, Google Ads and web work for the US clients of a New York agency.

Along the way I built websites on WordPress, Shopify, Wix and other platforms, and moved deep into paid ads. As a freelancer I now run marketing for 9Cork, a B2B cork brand, and Yogable, a US yoga e-commerce brand.

Today I lead performance marketing at Digi Maniac in Kurukshetra, train students on the same playbook, and run my own pet blog, Feed My Fur, on the side.
